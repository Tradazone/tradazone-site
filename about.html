About us

We are committed to empowering financial freedom across Africa through the transformative potential of cryptocurrency.

We've observed that many African entrepreneurs face significant challenges in conducting business with international clients; both within Africa and globally. According to the World Bank, African businesses face some of the highest remittance costs globally, with an average transaction fee of 8.2%, far above the global average of 6.3%.

These costs, combined with currency exchange complexities and excessive government taxes, create significant barriers for entrepreneurs seeking to expand internationally.

Additionally, a report by McKinsey highlights that only 20% of African small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) have access to trade finance, further impeding the challenges of operating internationally. These issues make it difficult for many entrepreneurs to receive payments from international clients, leading to a loss of trust and missed opportunities for collaboration.

At Tradazone, we believe in breaking these barriers and have developed a solution to address the pain points of our platform. For example, cryptocurrency transactions reduce cross-border payment costs by up to 90% compared to traditional methods, according to a study by Deloitte.

Our solution enables entrepreneurs to send and receive payments seamlessly while offering additional features designed to enhance their business operations and overall experience.

Tradazone is here to empower entrepreneurs, helping them overcome financial limitations and unlock new opportunities in the global marketplace.
