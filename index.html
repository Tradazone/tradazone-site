How it works

From signup to paid in three steps.

1

Connect your wallet

Sign in with MetaMask, Freighter, Lobstr, Argent, or Braavos. No registration form, no password.

2

Create an invoice or checkout

Add line items, set an amount, and choose which wallet address receives the payment.

3

Get paid on-chain

Share the payment link. Your customer pays with their wallet. Funds land directly in yours — no intermediary.

Multi-chain by design

Your customers pay on their preferred chain. You receive funds on yours.

Ethereum

EVM-compatible. Accept ETH via MetaMask, Phantom, Coinbase Wallet, and any EIP-6963 provider.

Stellar
